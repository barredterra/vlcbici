vlcbici

Plan a trip across Valencia from door to door: walk to a Valenbisi station that has bikes, ride, return the bike at a station with free docks, and walk to your destination.

What it does

  • You enter a start and a destination: an address, a point on the map or your current location.
  • The app loads live Valenbisi station data and picks the nearest pickup station with 3 or more bikes and the nearest return station with 3 or more free docks.
  • It shows the walk, the ride and the second walk together on the map, with distance, time and turn-by-turn directions. If the bike does not help, it shows only a walking route.
  • During the ride, it checks the stations every minute and tells you when your pickup station runs out of bikes or your return station runs out of docks.

Why it exists

I spent a workation in Valencia and got around by Valenbisi. The official Valenbisi app shows the stations, but it does not plan a route. I did not notice that Google Maps has a similar feature. As an occasional OpenStreetMap contributor, I asked Claude, an AI assistant, to put the city's open station data on an OpenStreetMap map and to build a small interface for it. This app is the result.

Open source, no tracking

Unlike Google Maps, vlcbici is open source and does not track you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no cookies, and it stores nothing about you. Your location stays in your browser until you plan a trip.

To plan a trip, your browser sends the addresses that you type to the Nominatim geocoder, and the start, stations and destination to the OSRM routing server. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. These are public community services. The app has no backend of its own: its web server only delivers the app files.

The code is on GitHub under the MIT License.

Data

  • Station availability and bike network: Valencia Open Data, CC BY 4.0.
  • Map: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Cycling map style by CyclOSM.
  • Routing: OSRM by FOSSGIS. Geocoding: Nominatim.

Unofficial app. Not affiliated with Valenbisi, JCDecaux or the City of Valencia. Station data can be out of date. Check the station before you rely on it. You unlock the bike in the official Valenbisi app.
